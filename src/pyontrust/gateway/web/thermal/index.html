🎯 Calibration (one-point reference)

The Seek Compact reports raw sensor counts, so absolute temperatures are estimates. Aim the camera at something of known temperature (e.g. a wall you know is ~24 °C), enter that value below and press Calibrate — all readings are then shifted so the frame median matches your reference. The offset persists across restarts and applies to every consumer (this dashboard, FlowLab blocks).
